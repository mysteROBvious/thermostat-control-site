Thermostat Control

A personal iPhone app. It isn't sold or offered to the public.

Thermostat Control shows and adjusts the thermostats in one household from a single screen:

  • Google Nest thermostats, through Google's Smart Device Management API, after the owner grants access on Google's Nest permissions page.
  • Thermostats and mini-split controllers that are in Apple Home, through Apple's HomeKit, after the owner allows Home access on the iPhone.

The app changes a thermostat only when its user taps a control. It has no accounts, servers, ads, or analytics.
